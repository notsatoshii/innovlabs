### 3. What we're not

home.not.headline: Korea is second in the world at paying for AI. But not even top 10 at using it.*
home.not.sub: The subscriptions got bought. The second half never got taught. Here's what filled the gap.

home.not.money.title: "Make money with AI"
home.not.money.body: Sells you an outcome and skips the skill. When the trend moves on, you're left with a course and no habit.

home.not.fluff.title: "What is AI?"
home.not.fluff.body: Explains the technology without touching your work. You leave informed and exactly as slow as you arrived.

home.not.bootcamp.title: "Build a product with AI"
home.not.bootcamp.body: Teaches you to ship an app. Useful, if you wanted an app. Most people wanted their afternoon back.

home.not.us.title: Not prompts. Workflows.
home.not.us.body: AI that runs inside the work you already do. We teach the setup, the habits, and the judgment, using the same tools we run our own companies on.

home.not.footnote: * Paid subscribers: second globally, OpenAI, June 2025. Usage: outside the top 10 countries for ChatGPT web traffic, SimilarWeb via Asia Economy, August 2025. Downloads: 21st globally, Sensor Tower, 2026.
